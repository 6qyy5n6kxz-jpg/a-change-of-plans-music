About the duo

Familiar songs, fresh arrangements, two distinct voices

A Change Of Plans is Devin Frank and Kendra German. Devin moves between piano and guitar while sharing lead vocals with Kendra, whose expressive vocal style expands the duo's range across country, pop, rock, singer-songwriter favorites, and classics. The result is a flexible show that can support conversation in an intimate room or bring more energy to fairs, festivals, community events, and private gatherings.
